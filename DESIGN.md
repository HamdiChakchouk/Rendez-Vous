# Reservy Design System

## 1. Brand Identity
- **Name:** Reservy
- **Vibe:** Clean, Modern, Professional, Accessible
- **Industry:** Beauty, Wellness, Barbershops, Hair Salons

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary Color:** `#1152d4` (A vibrant, trustworthy blue)
- **Secondary / Accents:** 
  - Dark Gray/Black `#111827` for primary text and dark backgrounds
  - Light Gray `#F3F4F6` for borders and subtle backgrounds
  - Mid Gray `#9CA3AF` for inactive icons and secondary text
- **Background:** White `#FFFFFF` for the main application background.

## 3. Typography
- **Primary Font:** Manrope (Clean, geometric sans-serif)
- **Headings:** Bold (`700`), large sizes for strong hierarchy
- **Body Text:** Regular (`400`) and Medium (`500`), highly readable

## 4. UI Elements
- **Corner Roundness:** 8px (`ROUND_EIGHT`) for cards, buttons, and inputs.
- **Buttons:** 
  - *Primary:* Solid blue background (`#1152d4`), white text, bold font, rounded corners.
  - *Secondary:* Transparent background, outline or distinct border.
- **Icons:** Minimalist, consistent stroke weight (Lucide Icons style).

## 5. Components & Navigation
- **Bottom Navigation Bar:** White background, thin top border (`#F3F4F6`). Active icons are Primary Blue (`#1152d4`), inactive icons are Mid Gray (`#9CA3AF`).
- **Cards:** White background with subtle shadows or light gray borders, used for salon listings and appointments.
- **Overlays:** Dark transparent overlays (`rgba(0,0,0,0.45)`) on top of hero images to ensure text readability (e.g., on the Landing Screen).

## 6. Layout
- **Spacing:** Generous padding and margins to let elements breathe.
- **Structure:** Clear separation between sections, utilizing visual hierarchy to guide the user's eye towards primary actions (like "Réserver").
